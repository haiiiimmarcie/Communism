# Communism
Communism is the doctrine of the conditions of the liberation of the proletariat. its goal is to organise society in such a way that every member of it can develop and use all his capabilities and powers in complete freedom and without thereby infringing the basic conditions of this society - This is achieved by the elimination of private property. [<sup>`#1`</sup>](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1847/11/prin-com.htm#:~:text=Communism%20is%20the%20doctrine%20of%20the%20conditions%20of%20the%20liberation%20of%20the%20proletariat.), [<sup>`#2`</sup>](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1847/06/09.htm#12:~:text=Question%202%3A%20What,community%20of%20property.)
